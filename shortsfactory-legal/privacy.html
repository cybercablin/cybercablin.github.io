Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. What we collect

When you connect a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account, the App receives only what the platform's authorization returns: your public profile name and avatar, an account identifier, and access/refresh tokens needed to upload videos for you. The App does not read your private messages, followers, or watch history.

2. How we use it

The data is used only to show which account is connected and to upload videos you or the owner approve. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens are stored encrypted in the database of the App on the owner's own infrastructure. Videos and project data are stored on that same infrastructure.

4. Third-party services

To create videos the App may send the text of the script to AI and text-to-speech providers. No platform tokens or personal account data are sent to them. Publishing happens through the official APIs of TikTok, YouTube (Google) and Instagram (Meta), whose own privacy policies apply to the data they hold.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the account is connected. You can disconnect at any time from the App, which deletes the stored tokens, and you can also revoke access from your TikTok, Google or Instagram security settings. To request deletion of any other data, contact us.

6. Security

Access tokens are encrypted at rest and the App can be protected with a password. No system is perfectly secure, but we apply reasonable measures.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
